Developmental Cell 2025 · Nature Plants Research Highlight

Self-organization of Vascular Strands in the Arabidopsis Shoot Apex

Agata Burian, Andrzej Kokosza, Magdalena Raczyńska-Szajgin, Pavel Solansky, Marja Timmermans, Wojtek Pałubicki

Vascular strands in the shoot apex are initiated inside the vascular cylinder rather than by surface auxin, yet still follow the phyllotactic pattern. A model of mutually repelling strands reproduces the observed spacing and the 137° divergence angle, revealing self-organization behind vascular patterning.
